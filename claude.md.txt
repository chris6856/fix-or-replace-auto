V1 Recommendation States

I'd use four rather than three:

🟢 FIX IT

Economics favor repairing.

🟡 GET ANOTHER QUOTE

Keeping the vehicle may make sense, but the repair price is too close to the threshold.

🔴 REPLACE IT

Replacement economics now look stronger.

⚪ TOO CLOSE TO CALL

Neither option has a meaningful financial advantage.

That last state builds credibility. The app shouldn't pretend every decision has an obvious answer.
