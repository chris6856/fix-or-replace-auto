Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy explains what Fix or Replace Auto ("we," "us," "the app") collects when you use our mobile application and website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We built this app to help you make a clearer financial decision about your vehicle — this policy is written in that same spirit: plainly, and without more jargon than necessary.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (handled securely by our authentication provider, Supabase). If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google instead, we receive basic profile information (such as your name and email) from that provider, governed by their own privacy policies.

Vehicle information

You may provide, for each vehicle in your garage:

  • VIN (vehicle identification number), year, make, model, trim, and nickname
  • Mileage, ZIP code, primary driver, condition, and reliability history

Repair and financial information

To generate a fix-or-replace recommendation, you provide information such as:

  • Repair descriptions and estimated costs from your shop
  • Recent repair history and any outstanding loan payoff amount
  • Replacement vehicle price, taxes, fees, trade-in value, and financing terms you enter

This information is used only to calculate and store your decision history — we do not use it for any purpose beyond providing the app's core features to you.

Camera access

With your permission, the app uses your device's camera to scan a VIN barcode or read VIN text directly from your vehicle. This processing happens on your device; photos are not uploaded to or stored on our servers, and are used only to populate the VIN field.

Usage and technical information

Like most apps, we may collect standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash or error logs, to help us maintain and improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate and display your fix-or-replace recommendation and save your decision history
  • To look up an estimated market value for a vehicle, when you provide a VIN
  • To generate a plain-language explanation of your results
  • To maintain your account and provide customer support
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the app

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with the following service providers, only as needed to provide the app's features:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and hosting provider. Stores your account, vehicle, and decision data.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes repair descriptions and calculated results to generate structured data and plain-language explanations. Anthropic does not receive your account credentials or contact information.
  • MarketCheck — receives a vehicle's VIN, mileage, and ZIP code to provide an estimated market value.
  • NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration) — a free public U.S. government service used to decode a VIN into vehicle specifications.
  • Apple and Google — if you choose to sign in using "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google," those providers authenticate you according to their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and vehicle data for as long as your account remains active, so your decision history stays available to you. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (below).

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to receive a copy of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children's Privacy

Fix or Replace Auto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, including industry-standard encryption in transit and row-level access controls on our database. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at privacy@fixorreplaceauto.com.

This policy describes the app as designed and built. If any feature described here changes before launch, this page should be updated to match before the app goes live.
